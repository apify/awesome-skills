## List shape

349 lists total: 103 verticals across four stages (`pre_seed`, `seed`, `series_a`, `series_b`), plus 14 geographic lists and a handful of other groupings. Slug shape is `<sector>-<stage>`, for example `fintech-seed`.

Largest lists: `fintech-seed` 8775, `san-francisco-bay-area` 8246, `fintech-series-a` 6618, `enterprise-seed` 6418, `saas-seed` 5778, `ai-seed` 5390, `new-york-city` 4306.
